# Soldering Practice Kit Project
This project stems off the Soldering Practice Kit made by Elenco. (Model Sp-1A)

## The Kit
The kit first starts with soldering practice of the back of the board. After working over basic soldering practices and safety, it took me through soldering on pads, bridging and unbridging solder, and finally desoldering using a solder wick and or a solder sucker.
